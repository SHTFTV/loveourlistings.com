Luxury realtors, tell us who you are.

Looking for more exposure for your work? Love Our Listings wants to work with realtors who know their luxury markets firsthand. Introduce yourself and show us one property, block or neighbourhood you could tell a great story about.

Show us what only a local would notice

Think about the detail you point out on a second showing. Maybe the view changes when you step off the terrace. Maybe the architect kept an old orchard that explains why the house sits at an angle. What would a reader miss in the listing photos, and how did you come to know it?

Tell us where you fit

Send your name, brokerage, the markets you serve and a link to your public profile. Choose one listing or portfolio example and give us your pitch in a few sentences. Tell us whether you’d like us to consider an agent profile, a property feature or an ongoing collaboration.

If you’d like us to consider photos or video, start with a link. Name the photographer or filmmaker and let us know who can authorize editorial use. For an off-market home, share only what the owner has cleared for discussion.

Introduce yourself to the editors

Write to love@loveourlistings.com. Your introduction goes privately to our editorial and partnerships team. We’ll discuss permissions, credits and any proposed partnership terms with you before a feature goes live.
