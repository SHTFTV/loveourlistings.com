We Love This Listing: A Three-Acre Equestrian Estate in Coto de Caza

Horse facilities anchor this Orange County estate, where Robb Report describes a renovated main residence, separate guest house and generous spaces for gathering.

Unpublished editorial preview — awaiting visual QA. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

The Horses Come First

According to Robb Report, the three-acre property includes a four-stall barn with a tack room, office and saloon, alongside a riding arena and three pastures. That combination gives the equestrian identity substance: this is not simply a large house borrowing ranch style.

Rustic, Without Being One-Note

Robb Report dates the original construction to 1981 and notes subsequent renovations. Its description of the 8,400-square-foot main residence pairs cathedral ceilings and exposed beams with a neutral palette. The appeal is that balance: substantial architectural gestures, with finishes that sound comparatively restrained.

Room for Different Gatherings

The publication reports dual stone-top kitchen islands, a living-room wet bar and a temperature-controlled wine cellar with approximately 1,700-bottle capacity and a dedicated tasting room. Read together, these suggest several distinct settings for hosting, rather than one oversized entertaining space expected to do everything.

A Separate Place to Stay

Robb Report also describes a one-bedroom guest house with an open-plan living room, dining room and kitchen, plus access to a sauna and steam room. Editorially, that separate accommodation is a compelling counterpoint to the main residence’s scale: room for company without making every gathering communal.
