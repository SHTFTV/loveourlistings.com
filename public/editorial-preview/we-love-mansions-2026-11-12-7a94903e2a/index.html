We Love This Listing: A Palm Beach Mansion With a Courtyard at Its Heart

Robb Report’s account of Fran Tarkenton’s Palm Beach purchase reveals a Mediterranean-style home balancing ornate interiors with courtyard living.

Unpublished editorial preview — awaiting visual QA. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

Scale With Structure

According to Robb Report, the retired quarterback acquired a roughly 8,700-square-foot residence on Palm Beach’s Billionaires Row. The publication describes five bedrooms across two levels. What interests us is how that generous scale accommodates distinct spaces for gathering and retreat, rather than relying on size alone.

A Rich Material Palette

Robb Report identifies imported French wooden inlay and marble floors, pecky cypress coffered ceilings, and French doors throughout. Those reported details suggest a design built around texture and craftsmanship. The contrast between patterned wood and stone gives the interiors a more specific identity than the mansion label conveys.

A Kitchen for Company

The kitchen includes a La Cornue range, a U-shaped island seating 10, and a breakfast nook, the publication reports. Editorially, that combination is compelling: it provides more than one setting for an informal meal, alongside the home’s separately reported formal dining room.

The Courtyard Counterpoint

Outside, Robb Report describes a central courtyard with a pool and spa beside an open-air loggia with a fireplace. That arrangement is the feature’s strongest counterpoint to the ornate interiors: an outdoor gathering space with its own architectural focus, not simply an expanse of lawn.
