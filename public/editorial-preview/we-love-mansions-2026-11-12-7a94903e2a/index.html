We Love This Listing: A Palm Beach Mansion With a Courtyard at Its Heart

Reportedly purchased by Fran Tarkenton, this Mediterranean-style home pairs elaborate interiors with a pool courtyard and ocean access across South Ocean Boulevard.

Unpublished editorial preview — awaiting visual QA. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

A Winter Retreat on a Grand Scale

According to Robb Report, retired quarterback Fran Tarkenton purchased the roughly 8,700-square-foot Palm Beach residence for $52 million. The publication describes a Mediterranean-style home with five bedrooms on a 0.67-acre parcel. Beyond those headline numbers, the courtyard and richly worked interiors offer more interesting reasons to linger.

Materials Set the Tone

Robb Report details imported French wooden inlay and marble floors, pecky cypress coffered ceilings, and French doors. An ornate living room adds a carved white marble fireplace. In editorial terms, the appeal is the contrast between pale stone and textured wood: a decorative vocabulary with more character than sheer scale alone.

A Kitchen Built Around Gathering

The kitchen includes a La Cornue range, a whitewashed brick backsplash, and a U-shaped island seating 10, the report says. That unusually generous island reads as a social centerpiece, giving the room a distinct role alongside the reported formal dining room.

Courtyard First, Coast Beyond

Outside, Robb Report describes a central courtyard with a pool and spa beside an open-air loggia with a fireplace. It also reports gated beach-access steps across South Ocean Boulevard—an important distinction from an uninterrupted house-to-sand setting. The courtyard, rather than the coastline alone, is what makes this arrangement compelling.
