We Love This Listing: Art and Water at Barefoot Beach

A gallery-linked guest wing and a top-floor lap pool distinguish this Gulf-front Florida home.

Unpublished editorial preview — awaiting visual QA. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

Two waterfront connections

According to Robb Report, this four-story home in Barefoot Beach occupies roughly half an acre, with 120 feet of direct beachfront. The property also includes a canal-front boat slip and lift. For us, that combination is the waterfront story: the beach and boating each have a distinct place in the property’s design.

Art along the way

Robb Report describes a two-bedroom guest wing connected to the main house by an art gallery, with its own kitchen, living room and elevator. The appeal is architectural as much as decorative: a gallery turns the connection between shared and separate quarters into a space with a purpose of its own.

A top-floor departure

The publication places a 36-foot lap pool and spa on the top floor overlooking the Gulf, alongside a cabana and outdoor kitchen. That top-floor arrangement is the feature we find most distinctive. It gives swimming and outdoor entertaining an elevated setting rather than treating the pool as an automatic ground-level fixture.

Beyond the showpieces

Robb Report also details 103 support pilings, extensive waterproofing and drainage systems, and a whole-house backup generator. These reported specifications are not proof of storm-proof performance. They do, however, add a practical counterpoint to the gallery and pool—the less theatrical side of an unusually ambitious coastal home.
