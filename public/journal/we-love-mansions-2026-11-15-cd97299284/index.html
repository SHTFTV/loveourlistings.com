We Love This Listing: The Georgian house behind Nanteos’s hotel years

Near Aberystwyth, a former country house hotel comes with a stable court, a lake and roughly 31 acres.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-15.

With thanks

Savills via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Read the front elevation

Ivy covers part of Nanteos, yet the doorway remains easy to pick out beneath its columns. The wide approach gives a visitor time to take in the arrangement of windows before reaching the steps. Country Life featured the estate on November 15 at £3.5 million. Savills identifies the house as Grade I listed and describes a Georgian staircase and a later Rococo saloon.

Look beyond the bedroom count

The estate has operated as a hotel and wedding venue. Its scale becomes easier to understand when the accommodation is separated into its parts. Savills lists 23 bedroom suites alongside a four-bedroom cottage in the coach house and stable complex. Outside, the walled garden, woodland and lake occupy a landscape of about 31 acres.
