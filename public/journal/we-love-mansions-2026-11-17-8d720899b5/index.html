We Love This Listing: Two gardens offer different views at Chelsea’s Van Ness House

At ground level this brownstone has a limestone-paved garden. The rooftop garden looks towards Saint Peter’s Church.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

With thanks

Gloria Kilbourne via ELLE Decor

First published by ELLE Decor. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A garden behind the house

The first garden belongs to the quieter side of the brownstone. Limestone paving leaves space for linden trees, whose foliage turns gold in autumn. The second garden occupies the roof and looks towards Saint Peter’s Church. In a house that extends across seven levels, they give someone two quite different reasons to step outside. ELLE Decor featured the Van Ness House in its November 17 report, citing a $21.5 million asking price through Compass.

Reading the walls

The Chelsea house dates to the middle of the nineteenth century. It was enlarged over time and now has five bedrooms across 7,800 square feet. Designer Miles Redd worked with its existing plaster details. In Gloria Kilbourne’s photograph of the sitting room, framed pictures and a mirror hang among flowering branches on pale blue wallpaper. The oak flooring has its own pattern underfoot, laid in chevrons and fitted with heat.
