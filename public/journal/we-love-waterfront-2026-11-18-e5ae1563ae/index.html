We Love This Listing: La Gorce Island’s Broad Bayfront Perspective

A 1.23-acre Miami Beach compound places its extensive Biscayne Bay frontage at the centre of the design.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

The waterfront defines the story

Coldwell Banker Global Luxury’s November 18 report describes a 1.23-acre compound on La Gorce Island with 320 feet of Biscayne Bay frontage. The property is being marketed at $170 million; that is an asking price, not a completed sale. Its position at the island’s tip gives the waterfront an unusually prominent role.

Scale with an outdoor focus

The brokerage describes more than 18,000 square feet, eight bedrooms, a glass staircase and a primary retreat with its own balcony. Outside, a covered terrace, infinity-edge pool and circular spa extend the living spaces toward the bay. Deep-water access is also reported, with no fixed bridges along the route.

Why we love this listing

For us, the compelling detail is how a wide water boundary can shape the whole experience of a home. The terraces and glazing put the setting ahead of a simple count of rooms, making this a distinctive waterfront feature. Want to see your property here?
