We Love This Listing: A Cowes chapel with a working waterfront past

At Alexandra House, the chapel windows survive and the river runs right past the building.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

Between street and harbour

Stand on the street side of Alexandra House and the clues are all there. Three arched upper windows sit beneath a pediment, with a glimpse of blue water down the side of the building. Spence Willard places the property on the sheltered western bank of the Medina. Country Life reported a £1.65 million asking price on November 13.

A project with a history

The building began as a Wesleyan chapel in 1831. Boatbuilder Sam Saunders later used it as a workshop. That sequence makes the deep-water pontoon more than a picturesque extra. Water access once helped explain why people worked here. For a buyer today, the starting point is the shell and the berth.
