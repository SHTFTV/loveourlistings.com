We Love This Listing: Celine Dion’s Former Jupiter Mansion

A Mediterranean-style estate pairs theatrical interiors with a private dock—a combination more compelling than its celebrity provenance alone.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-10.

A Musical Beginning

According to Robb Report, this Eagle Drive mansion in Jupiter was built in 1998 for Celine Dion and her late husband, René Angélil. The publication identifies its style as Mediterranean. For us, that origin provides an intriguing introduction, rather than the sole reason to give the property attention.

Scale With a Counterpoint

Robb Report puts the house at approximately 14,750 square feet on roughly two-thirds of an acre. Those figures offer an interesting contrast: a substantial mansion without an acreage-led identity. Its appeal, on paper, rests more on the spaces within and their relationship to the water.

A Room That Embraces Drama

The private theater features red velvet seating, a coffered ceiling and a crystal chandelier, the publication reports. Our favorite detail is that unabashedly theatrical combination. Rather than treating entertainment as a purely technical amenity, the room’s reported decorative elements give it a distinct visual personality.

The Waterfront Chapter

Outside, Robb Report describes a pool and hot tub overlooking the water, a covered patio with an outdoor kitchen, and a private dock providing waterway access. The dock is the standout for us: it adds a practical boating dimension alongside the estate’s more exuberant indoor features.
