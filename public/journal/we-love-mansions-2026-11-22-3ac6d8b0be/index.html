We Love This Listing: A postwar house in Cotswold dress at Damsell Cross

Near Painswick, a seven-bedroom house takes its cues from the local stone buildings and opens onto a south-facing terrace.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

Dating the gables

From above, the roofs at Damsell Cross House seem to belong to a much older building. Gables of different heights break up the long frontage, and mature trees shelter the approach. Country Life dates construction to 1955 in its November 22 Gloucestershire selection. The house is a chance to see how a postwar designer used the familiar outline of a Cotswold house at a scale big enough to accommodate seven bedrooms.

Which staircase to take

The two ends of the plan serve different sorts of evenings. Knight Frank’s particulars put the dining room, drawing room and study off the reception hall. The sitting room and kitchen lead towards the south terrace instead. Upstairs there is a laundry beside the back stairs, a small detail to appreciate in a house this large. A pool is visible beyond the gables in the photograph. Country Life reported an asking price of £2.25 million. Want to see your property here?
