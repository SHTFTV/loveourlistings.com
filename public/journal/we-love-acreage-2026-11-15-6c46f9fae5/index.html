We Love This Listing: Two approaches to Top Farm in Laverton

A path to the front door and a drive to the barn tell different stories about this Cotswold farmhouse.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-15.

With thanks

Jackson-Stops via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Arriving on foot

A photograph taken above Top Farm shows how the farmhouse, detached barn and smaller outbuilding sit along the edge of open grass. Jackson-Stops describes two different ways to approach the house from the lane in Laverton. A path planted with lavender leads visitors to the front door. The drive gives someone arriving with muddy boots or something heavy to unload a shorter way into the courtyard beside the barn.

Where the work goes

It is easy to see the barn as an accessory to the six-bedroom farmhouse. Read the particulars and it becomes clear how much of the place depends on it. The barn holds a tool store, wood store, garage and gym. Out beyond the garden are an orchard and paddock, part of just under eight acres in all.
