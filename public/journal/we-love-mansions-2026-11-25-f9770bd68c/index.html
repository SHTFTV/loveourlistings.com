We Love This Listing: Two arches give a Nunhead kitchen two gardens to look into

One tall window frames the garden at this Limesford Road house. A smaller opening leads into the courtyard.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-25.

With thanks

Aucoot via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A pair of openings

Stand at the end of the kitchen island in Aucoot’s photograph and the garden comes into view through a tall arch of glass. Immediately to the left is a narrower arch, just wide enough for a door. That one opens to a sheltered courtyard. The two openings give the kitchen a choice of outlook even on a day when the doors stay closed. Draper Studio handled the renovation of this Victorian terrace, with interiors by the owners’ practice, Studio Farra.

Light farther inside

The room behind the kitchen gets daylight from a glazed atrium. Farther towards the street, the old reception rooms still have their decorative plasterwork. Upstairs, the curved opening to the main bathroom repeats a shape you first see at garden level.
