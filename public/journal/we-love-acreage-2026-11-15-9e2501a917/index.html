We Love This Listing: The old estate laundry houses the kitchen at Home Farm

In Chawton, two former workrooms still shape the way this Hampshire farmhouse is used.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-15.

With thanks

Knight Frank via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The kitchen ceiling gives the game away

At Home Farm, the room where someone once tackled the estate washing now has space for a kitchen table. Knight Frank identifies it as the former laundry for the Chawton House estate. The pitched ceiling and exposed roof timbers survive, and a walk-in pantry keeps provisions within reach. Across the house there is another clue to its working past. The games room is believed to have served as the brewhouse.

Where a guest can withdraw

For a sense of the property’s layout, look at the photograph from above the garden. The roofline rises and falls as the buildings stretch along the terrace. Knight Frank describes a double reception room opening onto the south-facing terrace and, separately, Home Farm Barn with its own bedroom, bathroom and kitchen. Someone staying in the barn can join the household for supper and still have a space of their own the next morning.
