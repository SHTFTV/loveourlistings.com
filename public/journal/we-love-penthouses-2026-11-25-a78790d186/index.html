We Love This Listing: A Fisher Island penthouse with a garden on the roof

At Palazzo Della Luna, two kitchens serve the main living floor. A third place to cook sits among the trees on the roof.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-25.

With thanks

Jill Eber / 1 OAK Studios, as credited by Robb Report · original listing photograph via The Jills Zeder Group / Beaches MLS

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Dinner by the windows

At the dining table in Penthouse 2 at Palazzo Della Luna, guests sit beside glass that runs nearly the full height of the room. A cluster of little pendant lights hangs over the table. Across the wood floor, curved sofas form a second gathering place within sight of the first. Robb Report describes a Boffi kitchen with an L-shaped marble island serving this part of the home. There is also a separate catering kitchen for the work of feeding a crowd.

A garden above the apartment

Upstairs, a 7,300-square-foot roof terrace gives the five-bedroom duplex a patch of grass and mature trees above Fisher Island. A summer kitchen makes it possible to cook up here, and the heated pool has its own spa. We like how the two levels change the terms of an invitation. Dinner can mean a seat under those pendant lights or an evening outdoors looking across Biscayne Bay. Want to see your property here?
