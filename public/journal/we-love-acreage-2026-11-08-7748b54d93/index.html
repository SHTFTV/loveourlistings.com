We Love This Listing: Malpas farm

A renovated Cheshire farmhouse shares its grounds with three cottages. The acreage offered separately is a different proposition.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-08.

A view from the lawn

The first thing you notice in the Savills photograph is the tree. Its canopy frames the buildings across a stretch of lawn, and suddenly the distance between the house and its neighbours makes sense. From this angle the farm on the edge of Malpas still reads as a group of buildings with a shared history.

What comes with the farm

Country Life’s November 8 feature put the asking price at £4.95 million. The offering it described comprised the renovated home, three cottages and 22.7 acres. It also mentioned another 107 acres available separately. That distinction matters to anyone weighing up how much ground they actually want to take on.

Why we love this listing

Renovation may draw attention indoors, but this is one house where we would start the tour outside. The long view explains how all the pieces fit together. Want to see your property here?
