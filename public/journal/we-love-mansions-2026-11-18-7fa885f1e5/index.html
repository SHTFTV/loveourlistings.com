We Love This Listing: An Architect’s Apartment at Great Maytham Hall

The Burnett Suite brings generous rooms and a garden-linked literary history to a Kent country-house apartment.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

With thanks

Charlotte Day · via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Architecture at an intimate scale

Country Life’s November 18 feature places the Burnett Suite inside Great Maytham Hall in Rolvenden, Kent. The 1,481-square-foot apartment was Sir Terry Farrell’s home and occupies the central block of the country house reworked by Edwin Lutyens between 1909 and 1912. The reported asking price is £525,000.

Rooms with a landscape

Inside, the source describes a sitting room with ten-foot ceilings, a fireplace and sash windows looking toward the gatehouse, clock tower and parkland. A separate study faces the terraces; there is one double bedroom. Those details suggest a home organised around outlook and proportion, rather than an abundance of rooms.

Why we love this listing

The attraction is grand architecture at an apartment’s scale. Lift access and shared grounds give that story a different rhythm from an entire private estate.
