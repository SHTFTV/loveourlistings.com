We Love This Listing: A bedroom door opens onto the skyline at the Hit Factory

The upper floor of this Hell’s Kitchen duplex shares its level with more than a thousand square feet of private terrace.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

With thanks

Scott Wintrow/Gamut Photos via ELLE Decor

First published by ELLE Decor. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

From the roof back to the foyer

A group of chairs fits comfortably on the terrace in Scott Wintrow’s photograph, with space left to walk around them and look over the parapet. Doors lead back to the top-floor bedroom suite. Downstairs there are two more bedrooms and a double-height foyer lit from above. The arrangement gives the primary bedroom direct access to the largest outdoor space in the apartment.

An address with a recording history

The 1928 building on West 54th Street once housed the Hit Factory recording studio. The studio moved again in 2005, and the building became loft-style condominiums. Lady Gaga later lived in this 3,700-square-foot duplex. White oak ties the renovated rooms together, and the home includes an office as well as the three bedrooms. ELLE Decor reported on November 16 that Douglas Elliman had listed penthouse PHC at 421 West 54th Street for $6.995 million.
