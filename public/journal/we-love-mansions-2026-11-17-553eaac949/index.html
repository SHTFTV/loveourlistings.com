We Love This Listing: Stone archways still set the pace at Casa de la Parra

In Biniali, a Mallorcan village house built in 1900 opens onto a courtyard shaded by vines.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

With thanks

Fantastic Frank via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Reading a room through the arch

The stone at the edge of the sitting room is uneven enough to catch the light differently on each block. Step back and those blocks form a broad arch. The restoration of Casa de la Parra leaves these openings in place, so the original masonry still determines how people move between the sitting and dining rooms. A dark steel staircase picks up the route upstairs. The Spaces reported a €1.55 million asking price through Fantastic Frank on November 17.

The kitchen extends into the courtyard

At the front, shuttered windows and a timber door face the village street. The back of the house has a more private outlook. Here the kitchen opens to a courtyard where vines shade a second place to cook. Three bedrooms occupy the upper floor, each with a bathroom. In one, the warm colour comes from the terracotta-toned floor beneath a freestanding tub.
