We Love This Listing: Drift House gives the acreage a life of its own

Ponds, cottonwoods and a Snake River setting make this Jackson Hole property more than a house with a large plot.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-03.

The setting is the selection

Realtor.com’s November 3roundup led us to Drift House in Jackson, Wyoming. The matching Live Water Properties listing describes 42.6 acres with five ponds, mature cottonwoods and access to the Snake River. Architect Vince Lee designed the residence. These specific relationships between building and landscape place the property naturally in our Acreage collection.

Why we love this listing

The source photograph brings the home close to its surroundings: timber, trees and a reflection in the water share the frame. We like how the scene encourages attention to the spaces beyond the walls. On a property like this, a path, a change in light or a view across a pond can be as memorable as a formal room.

A landscape with several scales

Acreage is often summarized as a number. The more interesting question is how that space is experienced, from the outlook of the house to the places reached on foot. Here, the reported ponds and river corridor offer a clear starting point for that reading. The exact-property photograph and detailed description come from Live Water Properties; consult the original materials for boundaries, access and present listing terms.
