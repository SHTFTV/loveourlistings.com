We Love This Listing: A cherrywood library opens to the lawn on Jersey Road

In this west London townhouse, Selway Design Studio carried one timber finish from the dining seat to the garden doors.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

A seat at the back of the house

In the photograph of the library, someone sitting on the sofa has a choice between the books behind them and the garden a few steps away. American cherrywood links this end of the house to the dining banquette. Fold back the timber-framed doors and the library opens directly onto the southwest-facing lawn. The Spaces reported a £1.15 million asking price for the Jersey Road house on November 19.

Colour upstairs and down

At the front, the Edwardian sitting room keeps its sash windows, working fireplace and timber floor. The rear kitchen takes a different direction with stainless steel, marble and ceramic lights made by Annelie Fawke. Upstairs, three bedrooms have flowers painted directly onto the walls. Some of those rooms still have their fireplaces. The fourth bedroom is in the converted loft, where space has also been found for an office and a freestanding bath.
