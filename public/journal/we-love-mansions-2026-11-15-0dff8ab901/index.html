We Love This Listing: Blockley Victorian home

Six bedrooms and 4,760 square feet sit behind a remarkably restrained village facade in Blockley.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-15.

From the garden

Vaughan Reynolds’ photograph shows a warm-toned house above a green lawn. The windows line up neatly over three storeys, their rhythm broken at ground level by a small projecting porch. Trees frame the building on either side. It is an invitation to look at the proportions of the house before thinking about how big it is.

Village scale

Country Life included this Victorian home in its November 15 selection, reporting £2,295,000 through Vaughan Reynolds. There are six bedrooms in 4,760 square feet in the Gloucestershire village of Blockley. In the photograph, the height of the building does as much as its width to explain how that much space fits behind the front wall.
