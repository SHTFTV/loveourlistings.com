We Love This Listing: A long hearth anchors the living room at 1 Sanctuary Peak Court

In Henderson’s Ascaya community, a tall living room pairs a low fireplace with windows stacked above the entrance.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

With thanks

Rooted Elements / IS LUXURY

First published by Fancy Pants Homes. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Where to put the sofa

The striking thing in the listing photograph is the way the furniture faces two directions. From the long sofa, someone can look across to the fire set low in a wall of pale stone. From the chairs at the end, the view runs back through the room toward the garden. Tall windows above the entrance bring in daylight beyond the reach of the doors below. Even a room this tall has several comfortable places to sit.

Moving out onto the terrace

Fancy Pants Homes reported on November 11 that Brooke and Marco Hall had listed their Henderson home. The listing agent gives the address as 1 Sanctuary Peak Court. Built in 2021, the five-bedroom residence has retractable glass walls in the main living areas. They open directly onto the backyard, carrying the gathering space outside.
