We Love This Listing: A ramp slips beside the glass at a Genthod lakefront house

Outside Geneva, an understated approach to a multi-car garage leaves the sharply folded entrance of a lakeside house in view.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

Follow the paving

From the paved approach, the eye goes straight to a dark plane of metal pitched across the front of the house. A pale wall meets it on one side. On the other, a tall sheet of glass picks up the trees beyond the drive. The planted edge and low retaining wall are as important to this composition as the building materials. They give you a level line against which to read that extraordinary angle.

Two ways in

In a November 19 feature, Sotheby’s International Realty identifies the Genthod property as a lakefront home with a ramp leading to a garage for several cars. The photograph is taken outside, so the form of the entrance is much easier to see than the parking arrangement below. That suits the design.
