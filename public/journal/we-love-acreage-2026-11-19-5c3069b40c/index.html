We Love This Listing: A two-storey library at a Danbury estate

The main house at 125 Long Ridge Road brings its bookshelves, bedrooms and family spaces into conversation with the land outside.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

With thanks

Coldwell Banker Global Luxury · SmartMLS listing photograph

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The long view

In the listing photograph, a pale house stands at the end of an approach lined with trees. Woodland spreads out behind it, making the extent of this Danbury property easier to picture. Robb Report covered the 80-acre estate on November 19 and reported an asking price of $12.5 million through Cindy Raney of Coldwell Banker Realty.

A room worth lingering in

Inside, the library is the place we would start. Bookshelves line two storeys, giving one quiet activity an unusually generous share of the house. The family room offers a different experience, opening directly to a covered patio. Upstairs, four of the five bedrooms have Juliet balconies, while the principal bedroom has a full private balcony. Those distinctions matter: a door you can open for air serves a different purpose from a place to sit outside with a book. Want to see your property here?
