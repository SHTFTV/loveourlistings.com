We Love This Listing: Ceiling light reaches every panel at a La Quinta villa

A six-bedroom home in Benahavís puts its collector cars beneath a luminous grid, with a broad doorway opening straight onto the drive.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

With thanks

Spain Sotheby’s International Realty via Sotheby’s International Realty

First published by Sotheby’s International Realty. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The view from the driver’s seat

A pale convertible stands beneath a bright grid. The squares of light run from the back wall almost to the open doorway, where a second car is visible out on the drive. For a collector looking at paintwork, the size of that light source matters. Trace the line along the convertible’s bonnet in the photograph and you can see a broad reflection following its shape.

A room with a job

Sotheby’s International Realty included this La Quinta villa in a November 19 feature on privately owned car galleries. It describes a six-bedroom house in Benahavís with temperature control in the gallery. The wood-toned side walls keep the edges of the room relatively dark. From just inside the entrance, that gives the eye three things to follow through the space: the light overhead, the car in front and the patch of daylight at the end. Want to see your property here?
