We Love This Listing: A garden room disappears into the trees in Upper Nichols Canyon

A mirrored sauna and a stepped waterfall give the grounds of this Hollywood Hills house a life beyond the swimming pool.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

Following the planting

In the garden photograph, broad leaves crowd the edges of a small seating area, and branches hang low over its wooden doors. Elsewhere on the grounds, the sauna is clad in mirrors so the foliage appears to continue across its walls. The original house dates to 1997 and was designed by Jeffrey Mills. The Spaces reported on November 11 that OME DEZIN had redesigned the property and that the asking price was $14 million.

The house and pool share an edge

The main living area opens straight onto the pool. From there the grounds, which extend over an acre, offer several reasons to keep walking. A stepped waterfall, an outdoor kitchen and seats beside a fire pit serve different parts of the garden. The four-bedroom house has a quieter vantage point in the principal suite, where a corner terrace and a marble bathroom look towards the trees.
