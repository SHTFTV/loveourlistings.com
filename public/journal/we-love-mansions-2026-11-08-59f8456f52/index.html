We Love This Listing: Highfield House makes room for separate stays

An annexe and a guest wing give this Westoning house a flexible arrangement around garden-facing shared rooms.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-08.

Space to gather

The appeal of Highfield House is how its rooms connect. Michael Graham describes a kitchen, dining and family space opening to an outdoor entertaining area, alongside an orangery overlooking the garden. These shared spaces give a large house a clear centre of gravity.

Room for independence

The Westoning property also includes a one-bedroom annexe with its own kitchen and sitting room, plus a separate guest wing above the triple garage. The agent reports 6,161 square feet overall. That arrangement is particularly interesting for households that want company without sharing every daily routine.

Why we love this listing

Country Life featured the home on November 8 with a £1.75 million asking figure. For us, the more compelling story is the balance between generous gathering rooms and smaller places to withdraw. The wraparound garden brings the different parts together.
