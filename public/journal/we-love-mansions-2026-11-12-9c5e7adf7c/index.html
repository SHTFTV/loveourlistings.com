We Love This Listing: A Sedona neighbourhood’s old water tank now holds wine

At La Aguilera on Cibola Drive, two houses share an unusual piece of the neighbourhood’s history.

Published 2026-11-25. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

With thanks

Coldwell Banker Realty

First published by Fancy Pants Homes. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A second use for a steel tank

Before anyone thought of storing wine here, the tank at La Aguilera supplied water to homes in Cibola Hills. It went into service in the late 1950s and remained in use into the 1990s. Fancy Pants Homes reports that the owners later bought the empty tank from the water company for five dollars. Converting it took considerably more work. Between October 2009 and June 2010 the steel shell became a cellar and tasting room with space for around 1,600 bottles.

Outside the cellar

The listing covers two residences at 25 and 55 Cibola Drive. In the broker’s photograph, the terrace walls echo the red rock beyond the trees. The flat roofs step down the slope, and the decks have clear views into the woodland.
