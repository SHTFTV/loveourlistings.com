We Love This Listing: An Oxted shop counter stays put as the customers move out

At the former London Stores in Surrey, the old department store’s central stair still carries people between floors.

Published 2026-11-21. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

Where the shoppers went

Follow the broad wooden staircase through the centre of 79 High Street and it is easy to picture the building as a shop. T. Troughton London Stores once sold goods ranging from china to bed linen here in Oxted. Country Life featured its conversion into a five-bedroom house on November 13. John D Wood listed a guide price of £1.25 million. Two original counters remain, one on the ground floor and another on the first.

Life behind the counter

One counter now shares a thirty-foot sitting and dining room with exposed floorboards and wood panelling. It gives a visitor an immediate sense of scale. Imagine how much of a conventional living room would disappear beneath a shop fixture of that size. Upstairs, two bedrooms face the back garden. Delivery horses once occupied stables on that ground, close enough for a cart to carry purchases away from the shop.
