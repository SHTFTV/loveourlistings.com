We Love This Listing: A long curve shapes the Roundhouse at Cross in Hand

An East Sussex home by RX Architects pairs a curved facade with a sheltered outdoor gathering space.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

Follow the bend

At the Roundhouse near Heathfield, the curve begins on the outside and carries into some of the interior walls. Country Life credits the design to RX Architects. Polished concrete floors and exposed engineered timber beams give the eye two quite different surfaces to follow. The room that best explains the plan is the combined kitchen, dining and family space. At 39 feet by 21 feet, it opens to a covered outdoor area.

A smaller building in the garden

The four-bedroom house sits in a little over an acre. Among the grounds is a second, much older example of building in the round, a restored brick windmill base with a conical roof. We love that pairing. One curve organizes a busy family room, while the other encloses a separate retreat. Country Life included the property at £1.4 million in its November 13 selection.
