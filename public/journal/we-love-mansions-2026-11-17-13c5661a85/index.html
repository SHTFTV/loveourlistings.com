We Love This Listing: Guy Dreier’s Desert Geometry at Bighorn

At 642 Pinnacle Crest, stone, glass and sheltered outdoor rooms frame a distinctive Palm Desert home.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

A house shaped around its setting

Robb Report’s November 17 feature identifies 642 Pinnacle Crest as an 8,148-square-foot Guy Dreier design in Palm Desert. Egyptian limestone walls, French limestone floors and generous glazing give the desert setting a direct role in the architecture. The report names a $15.9 million asking price, with the home offered furnished through Bighorn Properties.

Private space within the larger plan

The four-bedroom arrangement includes a detached guest house. The primary suite has its own outdoor spaces, pool and gym, while the main grounds offer an infinity-edge pool and areas for outdoor dining. We find that separation more interesting than a simple list of amenities: the house provides several different scales of retreat.

Why we love this listing

Dreier’s sculptural vocabulary gives the property a recognisable identity, but its appeal also rests in the transitions between rooms and landscape. Stone, glass and protected terraces make that relationship the central subject. Want to see your property here?
