We Love This Listing: Grangewood House opens onto the High Weald

Terraced gardens and a conservatory make the setting part of this historic Fairlight home’s character.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-08.

A house with a long outlook

Grangewood House sits just outside Fairlight, where its conservatory looks across the High Weald. Strutt & Parker identifies the property as Grade II listed and describes stone elevations, tall sash windows and a vaulted entrance hall. Those details give the house a strong architectural identity before the garden comes into view.

The landscape unfolds

The agent reports 7.77 acres, with terraces stepping through the gardens toward a fountain, followed by meadow and woodland. A pool and a separate annexe above the garage add further places to spend time. The progression from formal planting to a looser landscape is what catches our attention.

Why we love this listing

Country Life included this East Sussex home in its November 8 selection at £3.1 million. Our favourite feature is the relationship between the rooms and the grounds: the outlook gives the interiors a subject beyond their own period details.
