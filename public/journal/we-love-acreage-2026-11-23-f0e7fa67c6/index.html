We Love This Listing: At Broadgate Farm, eight paddocks and two homes share ten acres

A farm track links this East Markham smallholding to the village bridlepath, while a second converted barn receives overnight guests.

Published 2026-11-25. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Fine & Country via Horse & Hound

First published by Horse & Hound. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Shelter in every field

Look beyond the gravel courtyard and you can see why the arrangement of the fields matters at Broadgate Farm. The ten acres are split into eight paddocks, each with its own shelter. A rider can follow the farm track out to a bridlepath leading to East Markham. Horse & Hound reports that permission has been granted for a full-sized riding arena. A buyer looking round today can inspect the fields and stables and decide where that next piece would fit.

What became of the barns

The main barn became a family home in 2020. Its kitchen shares an open room with the dining area, and a spiral stair goes up to a small library. The Old Dairy has a different role. Its five bedrooms have their own bathrooms, a practical arrangement for its use as holiday accommodation. Horse & Hound reported a £1.425 million asking price in its November 23 feature.
