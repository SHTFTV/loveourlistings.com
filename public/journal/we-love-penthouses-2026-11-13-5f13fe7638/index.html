We Love This Listing: Terracotta steps lead from a Hyères apartment to the rooftops

HeJu’s renovation gives a one-bedroom home in the old town a south-facing place to sit outdoors.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

With thanks

Archik via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Where the floor changes

The dining table sits under a timber ceiling in a room with pale walls and shelves recessed into the plaster. Rust-coloured tiles pick out the change in level. Follow them up and there is a terrace looking across the roofs of Hyères. In its November 13 report, The Spaces put the interior at 40 square metres and gave an asking price of €250,000 through Archik.

Four finishes in a small home

The apartment occupies the top floor of an early twentieth-century building near Villa Noailles. HeJu kept the darker ceiling beams in view above lighter wooden cabinetry. In the entrance, pink limewash follows the curve of the vault. Handmade tiles line the shower, and woven palm forms the headboard in the bedroom. Each has a different surface to catch the light. For a visitor walking in from the street, the changes in texture offer a way to remember the rooms in a compact floor plan.
