We Love This Listing: The arches of Cloud House bring a little Miami to Gospel Oak

In northwest London, architect Peter Morris drew on a neighbourhood church and a honeymoon in Florida to design a house for his family.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

UK Sotheby’s International Realty via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A roofline you can trace with a finger

A row of pink arches climbs towards the middle of the frontage and drops again at the far end. Blue sky shows in the tall windows. The colour came from a trip Peter Morris and Emily Kennedy took to Miami, while the arch shape owes something to nearby St Martin’s Church. The couple bought a former vicarage here and redesigned the site as two adjoining homes. The Spaces featured Cloud House on November 23 and reported an asking price of £2.5 million.

Room for work and a bath under the sky

The ground floor puts Morris’s studio alongside the kitchen, living and dining areas. Cupboard edges are scalloped and the kitchen is striped, details close enough to touch after seeing the bigger curves from the pavement. There is a bedroom and bathroom on each of the next two floors. On the roof terrace, a bathtub takes the daily routine of bathing outdoors.
