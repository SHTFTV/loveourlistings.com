We Love This Listing: A Cornish Lodge with Its Own Identity

Country Life’s account of The Lodge at Porthpean House reveals a compact coastal home with a thoughtfully divided plan.

Published 2026-11-25. Original source date: 2026-11-25.

With thanks

Country Life · John Bray & Associates

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The lodge, not the main house

Country Life identifies this property as the lodge in the grounds of Porthpean House, rather than the main house itself. That distinction is worth keeping centre stage: our interest lies in the smaller home’s own proportions and arrangement, not in borrowing grandeur from its neighbour.

An L-shaped starting point

According to the publication, The Lodge has an L-shaped plan, measures just over 1,000 square feet and contains three bedrooms and two bathrooms. To our minds, the interesting balance is between its modest overall size and the number of separate rooms—not a claim to sprawling country-house scale.

Together, and apart

Country Life also reports a large kitchen-diner and a separate sitting room. That is the layout detail we particularly like: cooking and dining share a setting, while sitting has a room of its own. The appeal is separation rather than an entirely open-plan arrangement.

A coastal connection

The beach is a few moments’ stroll away, Country Life reports, with St Austell a few minutes’ drive away. For us, that near-beach setting is the strongest thread in the story. It gives this compact lodge a coastal identity without requiring any claim to private shoreline.
