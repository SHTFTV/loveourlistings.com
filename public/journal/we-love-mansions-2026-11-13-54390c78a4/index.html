We Love This Listing: A mezzanine study in the old school at Badby

Beside a Northamptonshire village green, a former schoolhouse distributes three bedrooms across two very different levels.

Published 2026-11-21. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

A school on the green

A path between hedges leads to the stone entrance of the Old School House in Badby. The former school dates to the early nineteenth century and is Grade II listed. Country Life included it in its November 13 property selection, and Jackson-Stops gave a guide price of £650,000 for the three-bedroom home on The Green. Look closely at the windows and you can see how little pieces of glass fit into their diamond-shaped leads.

Following the stairs

The kitchen shares the lower ground floor with one bedroom. The dining and sitting rooms occupy the level above, where a mezzanine provides a place to study. The remaining two bedrooms are upstairs. That arrangement gives someone working from home a choice between a desk closer to the living spaces and a separate room on another floor. Outside, the hedges screen a lawn edged with flowers and shrubs.
