Our oceanfront pick

A waterfront address tells you where a house sits. Its floor plan tells you what living there might be like. That distinction is why this Black Point residence is our oceanfront Real Estate We Love pick: the broker describes a linear arrangement running parallel to the coastline. Instead of treating the view as the reward at the end of a corridor, that approach makes the shoreline part of the organising idea. This is a Love Our Listings editorial selection, not a ranking of market demand or a recommendation to buy.

What is actually on offer

Tracy Allen of Coldwell Banker Realty advertises 4157 Black Point Road in Honolulu for US$7,895,000. The listing identifies three bedrooms, four full bathrooms and 4,087 square feet of living area on a 0.53-acre parcel, with a 1929 construction date. A den is described as a possible fourth-bedroom option; we have kept the advertised bedroom count at three. The property was marked For Sale when checked on October 28, 2026. Asking price and availability can change.

THE OWNERSHIP QUESTION

Read the coastline through the plan.

A sea view and the way rooms meet it are different qualities.

Character has a structural side

The broker highlights exposed timber trusses, vaulted shiplap ceilings and a lava-rock fireplace with a koa mantel. Those are more interesting editorial clues than a long list of luxury adjectives. They suggest a useful question for the accompanying film: does each material help the room hold its own beside the outlook? A strong coastal interior needs somewhere for the eye to rest as well as somewhere for it to travel. The original broker video is the evidence to examine, rather than an invitation to imagine that we have inspected the house.

Read beyond the view

For an interested buyer, the next conversation should move from the frame to the practicalities. Ask the listing representative about the condition of the building envelope, the history and scope of alterations, maintenance documentation and the costs attached to this particular coastal site. Obtain professional advice appropriate to the property before relying on general assumptions about oceanfront ownership. The listing also describes access to a community ocean-fed saltwater pool. That is a shared neighbourhood amenity, not a pool represented here as belonging exclusively to this home.
