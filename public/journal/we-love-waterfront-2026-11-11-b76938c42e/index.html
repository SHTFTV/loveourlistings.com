We Love This Listing: waterfront living beyond the celebrity name

Hassan Whiteside’s Miami Beach home puts a dock, an aquarium and a striking staircase into one unusually personal waterfront story.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

The waterfront detail worth noticing

Robb Report’s November 11 feature describes the former NBA player’s waterfront home with a 45-foot dock, a built-in aquarium and a red spiral staircase. These are reported details from the original publication. Together they give this property a more specific identity than a celebrity name or a sale headline could provide.

Why we love this listing

The dock is the feature that makes us pause. It suggests a home organized around time on the water, while the aquarium brings an entirely different encounter with it indoors. The red staircase adds another kind of energy: a practical connection between floors becomes part of the home’s personality. That combination is the appeal of this selection.

A photograph with its own date

The exterior image comes from Resident’s earlier report about the same 528 Lakeview Court property, credited there to Jill Eber, Melissa Barragan and 1 Oak Studios. It shows the pool and layered terraces at dusk. We use it as earlier listing photography, rather than evidence of the home’s condition today. Follow the current Robb Report story for the reported transaction; this magazine has not inspected the property or independently confirmed its present condition.
