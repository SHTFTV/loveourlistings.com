We Love This Listing: Four walled gardens and a stone farmhouse on Halfpenny Lane

At The Old Rib Farm near Longridge, the grounds divide into small outdoor rooms with different uses.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

With thanks

eXp via OnTheMarket, as credited by Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A place to stop on the way in

The windows across the front of The Old Rib Farm sit low over a strip of planting and clipped box. From the front path you can take in almost the entire stone elevation before opening the door. Country Life featured the Grade II listed Lancashire farmhouse on November 22, picking out the surviving stone windows and the Aga in the kitchen. The magazine reported an asking price of £1.2 million.

Behind the garden walls

The agent describes four separate walled gardens. One is a working courtyard with raised beds and a greenhouse. Elsewhere a covered terrace leaves room to eat outside when the weather turns. Those boundaries carry through to the accommodation on Halfpenny Lane. Four bedrooms belong to the main house, while the fifth is in a detached annexe with its own kitchen, living space and entrance.
