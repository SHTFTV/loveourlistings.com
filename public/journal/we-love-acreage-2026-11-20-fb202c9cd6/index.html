We Love This Listing: The pool follows a curve at Leuthold House in New Canaan

Allan Gelbin’s design sets glass, mahogany and sweeping rooflines among five acres of trees in Connecticut.

Published 2026-11-25. Original source date: 2026-11-20.

With thanks

Daniel Milstein Photography via ELLE Decor

First published by ELLE Decor. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A curve seen from the water

At the edge of the swimming pool, look up. The broad roof bends above the upper windows, and its warm underside is visible from the terrace. A pale wall turns another corner below. Photographer Daniel Milstein’s view captures the echo between these lines and the curve of the pool. Allan Gelbin designed the two-storey house, completed in 1966. Frank Lloyd Wright was his mentor.

A landscape beyond the pool

The grounds have a second body of water, a pond supplied by a spring. A one-bedroom guest house stands on the five acres, which also hold about 150 specimen trees. Inside the main house, stretches of glass bring those plantings into the rooms, where mahogany contributes a warmer surface. Hobbs Inc. and JMK Architects restored the five-bedroom house in 2014. ELLE Decor reported the listing at $6.2 million through Douglas Elliman on November 20.
