We Love This Listing: An opening on each side of a Miami living room lets the bay breeze through

On Belle Meade Island, the kitchen shares a view across the living room to Biscayne Bay.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

With thanks

Daniel Petroni / The Chad Carroll Group via Fancy Pants Homes

First published by Fancy Pants Homes. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A channel through the house

The view from the kitchen island at 1155 Belle Meade Island Drive takes in a tall shelving wall, a row of seats and a strip of blue water beyond the pool. There is glass on both sides of the main living space. Slide those walls back and air can travel from the entrance courtyard, through the room and out to the bay. Thamann Architecture + Design arranged the Miami residence in three wings, with landscaping by Christopher Cawley.

Cooking with a view

A green marble island marks the main kitchen. Behind the fitted cabinetry is a second space for preparation, so there is somewhere to put the pans when dinner moves outside. An outdoor kitchen serves the waterfront terrace, which is also accessible from the main-floor primary suite.
