We Love This Listing: Merv Griffin’s 40-Acre Desert Retreat in La Quinta

A Moroccan-style main house, guest pods and substantial equestrian grounds give this former celebrity estate a story beyond its famous owner.

Published 2026-11-12. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

A smaller piece of a much larger vision

According to Robb Report, Merv Griffin began assembling his La Quinta retreat in the 1980s. After subdividing 200 acres in 2005, he retained the original estate on 40 acres. That history gives the acreage a particular identity: this was the core of his desert escape, rather than a later addition to it.

A house with its own character

Robb Report describes a Moroccan-style main home and identifies the primary residence as a single-story, 5,409-square-foot building. Its combined living and dining area opens to a covered terrace. The architectural interest here is the relationship between those shared rooms and the outdoor space—not simply the estate’s overall scale.

Room for a different kind of guest

The publication also reports four round guest pods, a gate house and staff quarters. Editorially, the pods are an especially distinctive element: their separate forms suggest a compound organized around multiple retreats rather than one overwhelmingly large house.

Acreage with an equestrian purpose

The grounds include a 16-stall horse barn, a thoroughbred racing track and a citrus orchard, according to Robb Report. It also describes an infinity pool overlooking a 2.5-acre manmade lake. Together, those reported features make the land central to the estate’s character, not merely a generous buffer around the residence.
