We Love This Listing: A skylight follows the dining table in Santa Monica Canyon

At a remodeled 1949 residence, daylight reaches the dining area from the ceiling as well as from the garden.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

With thanks

Neuefocus Photography via Realtor.com

First published by Realtor.com. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Where the daylight lands

Pull a chair up to the wood dining table and there are two ways to look outside. Through the doors on one side is the garden pool. Above the table is a band of overhead glazing that lets sunlight reach farther into the room. A dark wall behind the kitchen island makes the arrangement particularly clear in the photograph. The table is in the light, while the cupboards and built-in appliances recede into shadow.

Space to stay a little longer

The five-bedroom Santa Monica house was built in 1949 and renovated in 2022. Its second-floor guest wing gives visitors a place apart from the primary suite. The latter connects to two offices, one with a way out to a roof deck overlooking the canyon. Realtor.com featured the 4,746-square-foot house on November 22 with an asking price of $6,898,000.
