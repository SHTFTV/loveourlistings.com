We Love This Listing: The Witter Estate’s Tudor Revival Character

A dramatic staircase, four connected floors and terraced gardens give this Upper Piedmont home a distinct architectural story.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

A carefully articulated entrance

Robb Report’s November 16 feature traces the Witter Estate to a Williams & Wastell design completed in 1930. The Upper Piedmont residence was commissioned by Jean Carter Witter and is now reported with an $8.25 million asking price. Its entrance pairs a hand-carved floating staircase with a two-storey leaded-glass window.

Rooms and gardens with distinct roles

The report describes four floors connected by an elevator, with formal reception rooms, a wood-panelled study and lower-level spaces for entertaining, wine and exercise. Outside, terraced gardens lead toward a black-bottom pool and spa. A detached garage supports separate studio accommodation, adding another layer to the property’s arrangement.

Why we love this listing

The attraction is the consistency of the architectural language, from the timbered exterior to the stair and detailed interiors. At the same time, the terraces and separate accommodation give the estate different places to gather or retreat. It is a house whose character rewards a closer look. Want to see your property here?
