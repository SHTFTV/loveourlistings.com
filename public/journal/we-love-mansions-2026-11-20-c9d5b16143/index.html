We Love This Listing: Curved cabinetry gives a Johns Island kitchen its shape

At 1030 Slow Water View, a rounded island and a pair of striped lamps make a kitchen worth walking through on the way to the screened porch.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-20.

With thanks

ArchPresence, as credited by Robb Report · native listing photograph via Maison Real Estate

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A place at the island

From a stool at the kitchen island, you can see the same soft curve repeated across the room. The island has rounded ends. Tall cabinets on the far wall turn gently at the corners, and two striped pendant shades hang above the counter. The pale marble behind the cooktop runs all the way up to the cabinetry. With so much going on overhead, the quiet rhythm of the wood floor gives the eye somewhere to rest.

From front porch to pool

American Vernacular designed this Johns Island home in the Kiawah River community. Its front porches have blue beadboard ceilings and pink-and-white marble underfoot. Past the kitchen, French doors in the teal living room lead to a screened porch. The shades there can be lowered on every side.
