We Love This Listing: The dining table has room to breathe at Compo Mill Cove

A double-height dining area and a kitchen island give a five-bedroom Westport house two places to gather for a meal.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

With thanks

Modern Angles via The Week

First published by The Week US. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The space above the table

At 52 Compo Mill Cove, the dining table gets a room with the height of two floors. Exposed beams carry the eye overhead and the table stands close to the kitchen, where people can pull up to the island instead. It is a useful division on a busy morning. Some can stay at the table while someone else sets out breakfast at the counter. The kitchen has a double refrigerator and a walk-in pantry.

The view from the lawn

The Week included the 2016 shingled waterfront house in its November 16 selection. In the photograph, four chairs sit below a low stone wall, with the long covered porch a few steps up behind them. A banquette and stone fireplace give the living room its own gathering place when the weather turns. The magazine reported an asking price of $6.8 million for the house on its nearly half-acre lot.
