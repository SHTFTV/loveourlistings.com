We Love This Listing: At Eppstein House, the woods are never far from the living room

Frank Lloyd Wright designed the low-slung Michigan house for Samuel and Dorothy Eppstein in a community outside Kalamazoo.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-26.

With thanks

JDG Innovative and Tim Hills via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A room seen from the sofa

Look across the living room and two bands of windows reveal the trees at different heights. One reaches down to the floor, with glazed doors among the panels. Around the corner, a shorter row leaves the wall beneath it free for furniture. From a seat near the centre of the room, the view follows you around both sides. Frank Lloyd Wright designed the three-bedroom house for Samuel and Dorothy Eppstein in Galesburg. It dates to 1953.

A small part of The Acres

The concrete-block house occupies two acres, but its setting extends much farther. Its neighbours share The Acres, a community of Wright houses set among some 70 acres of woodland and meadows. A recent restoration retained the footprint, which helps explain why the relationship between the rooms and the garden still reads so clearly. On November 26, The Spaces reported an asking price of $2.1 million through Signature Sotheby’s International Realty.
