We Love This Listing: An inglenook sets the table at Lower Carden Hall

A sandstone fireplace and panelled walls give the Cheshire hall’s dining room its proportions.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

The room around the hearth

At Lower Carden Hall, the dining room would be worth seeing before anyone put the table in. A broad sandstone inglenook takes up much of one wall. A beam crosses the ceiling above it, and a large window lights the timber floor from the other side. Savills describes an oak cruck frame in the house and dates its origins to the fifteenth century. The exposed timber does as much as the furniture to give these rooms their shape.

Across the cobbles

A former coachman’s cottage reached from the cobbled courtyard offers a different scale of accommodation. It has its own kitchen, two bedrooms and a sitting room with doors into a private garden. Country Life featured the Grade I listed hall near Malpas on November 22 at £2.65 million. Savills puts the whole property, including the paddocks and grounds, at about 5.2 acres.
