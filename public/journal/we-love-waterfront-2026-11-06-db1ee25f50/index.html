We Love This Listing: a Malibu home that looks up as well as out

An ocean outlook and a private observatory put two very different kinds of sky in the same property story.

Published 2026-11-09. Original source date: 2026-11-06.

Two ways to enjoy the setting

In its 2026-11-06 report, Robb Report describes a Malibu residence with an ocean outlook and a private observatory. That pairing is the reason for this coastal selection. The source calls it ocean-view; we are not turning that into a claim of direct frontage, beach ownership or shore access.

Why we love this listing

The unusual room is a useful starting point, but the real appeal is the routine it suggests: time outside, a changing horizon and somewhere to look beyond the familiar view. A distinctive feature earns its place when it changes how a home might be enjoyed, rather than merely extending an inventory.

The practical reading

We would ask how the observatory and outdoor areas relate to the spaces used every day. Equipment, access, condition and local constraints belong in a specific property review. Follow the original publication for the reported design details, asking terms and photography; confirm present availability with the listing professional.
