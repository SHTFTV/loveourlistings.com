Selling a Luxury Home in Abbotsford? Meet Raphaelle Beaudoin

Plan your Abbotsford luxury home sale with Raphaelle Beaudoin and learn how to put your property forward for a Love Our Listings feature.

LOVE OUR LISTINGS EDITORIAL · NOVEMBER 24, 2026

An Abbotsford acreage with a workshop and a hillside home with a view can attract very different buyers. If you’re preparing to sell, ask which recent sales support your asking price and which details will help buyers see what makes your property distinctive.

Raphaelle Beaudoin is the featured Abbotsford listing agent on Abbotsford.io and is with Pathway Executives Realty Inc. Speak with her about comparable sales, what to prepare before photography, and how to market your home.

Put your property forward for a Love Our Listings feature

If you’d like your home considered for Love Our Listings, ask Raphaelle to help put it forward for editorial review. A submission should include the public listing link once it’s live, approved listing photographs, and a short description of what makes the property worth a closer look. A restored timber frame, a sheltered dining courtyard, or a studio with its own entrance gives readers a concrete reason to explore the listing.

Talk through your Abbotsford listing plan

Call Raphaelle at 604-316-8938 to discuss selling your home and ask about submitting it for a feature. For more local selling resources, visit Raphaelle’s Abbotsford.io profile.
