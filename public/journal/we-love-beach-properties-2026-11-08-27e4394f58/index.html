We Love This Listing: connected pavilions on Kauai’s South Shore

Aepo Nei 18 uses a sequence of open living spaces to respond to its tropical coastal setting.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-08.

One home within a paired story

Realtor.com’s November 8feature introduces two neighboring homes in Kukui‘ula on Kauai’s South Shore. We are focusing on Aepo Nei 18, which the source specifically identifies by its connected-pavilion layout. The report places both homes’ construction in 2025 and credits the architecture to Brazil-based Architects+CO.

Why we love this listing

A pavilion layout gives movement between spaces a different feeling. The structure, planted edges and outdoor areas can become part of the experience of the house, rather than separate additions to it. We like the way the credited photograph lets those connections speak: rooflines, open rooms and the pool sit in one continuous composition.

Keep the property in focus

The featured image is the photograph explicitly captioned as Aepo Nei 18 in the original story, credited to Steven Lippman and homesbystevenlippman.com. It shows this particular home, with no generic beach image standing in for it. The South Shore location supplies the coastal context for our Beach Properties selection. Follow the original report for its description of the views, community and dated asking terms.
