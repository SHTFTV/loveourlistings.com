We Love This Listing: At Pentre Ucha, a stream runs from the garden into woodland

A stone cottage on the edge of Llansilin has terraces, a small waterfall and a separate stone annexe.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-09.

With thanks

Inigo via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The garden route

At Pentre Ucha a glazed door leads from the kitchen and dining room onto a timber terrace. From there, stone steps link different levels of the garden. The stream makes its own way through the grounds towards the woods. In its November 9 feature, The Spaces identified the house as a seventeenth-century Grade II-listed cottage overlooking the Cynllaith Valley and reported an asking price of £595,000.

Two places to stay

The sitting room has a substantial stone fireplace. Local celebrations once gave it a particular use. According to The Spaces, a nineteenth-century resident prepared traditional dishes for village festivities there. The two bedrooms upstairs each have a bathroom beneath the roof of the main house. Across the grounds, the annexe has two floors, its own bedroom and bathroom, and a kitchenette. Guests could make breakfast there before joining the rest of the household on the terrace.
