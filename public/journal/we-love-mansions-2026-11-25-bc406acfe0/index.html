We Love This Listing: At Greensboro’s Julian Price House, the plasterwork survived a change of use

The 1929 Tudor house on Fisher Park Circle served as an inn after its restoration. It sold on November 19.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-25.

With thanks

Chris Groch Photography via Realtor.com

First published by Realtor.com. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Look up from the sofa

In Chris Groch’s photograph, a wide arch frames the sitting room of the Julian Price House. The furniture is gathered close to the fireplace, but the first thing to catch the light is the ceiling. Ribs of molded plaster run across it in a repeating pattern. The inn once invited its guests to sit here and take in that detail. Behind the chairs, a bank of small windowpanes brings daylight almost to the floor.

Selling a house with guests

Charles C. Hartmann designed the eight-bedroom Tudor house for Julian Price in Greensboro. After restoring it, Eric and Michael Fuko-Rizzo operated it as an inn. That complicated the sale in one practical way. Listing agent Kay Chesnutt told Realtor.com that viewings had to fit around bookings because paying guests had locked rooms.
