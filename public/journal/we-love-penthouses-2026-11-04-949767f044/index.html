We Love This Listing: a Tribeca penthouse with two river horizons

At 111 Murray Street, a full-floor home and a pair of terraces give the Manhattan skyline room to unfold.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-04.

The view is part of the plan

Robb Report’s November 4 feature reports Fashion Nova founder Richard Saghian’s $31 million purchase of a Manhattan penthouse. The source identifies the full-floor home at 111 Murray Street and describes a pair of terraces overlooking the Hudson and East rivers. The linked Douglas Elliman listing identifies the residence as Penthouse One. The sale report and earlier listing have different dates and purposes.

Why we love this listing

A penthouse does not become interesting through height alone. Here, we like the way the source photograph frames the skyline as part of an ordinary living space: seating and tables remain in the foreground while the city fills the glass beyond them. The terraces extend that conversation outdoors, giving the home more than one way to meet its surroundings.

A specific home, not a generic skyline

The featured photograph comes from the exact Penthouse One listing linked by the original report. It is earlier property marketing photography, rather than a new image taken for this article. We have kept the picture focused on the living space and its view, with no celebrity portrait or unrelated tower image standing in for the home. Follow Robb Report for the reported purchase and the original listing for its description of the residence.
