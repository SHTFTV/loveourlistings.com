We Love This Listing: Under a Westlake Hills home, the garage takes the long view

A hilltop Austin property puts 9,000 square feet of vehicle space below the house and gives the cars a wood-lined room of their own.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

With thanks

Sotheby’s International Realty

First published by Sotheby’s International Realty. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A room below the view

It is hard to judge the size of a garage from its entrance at the best of times. At this gated hilltop home in Austin, the approach gives particularly little away. Sotheby’s International Realty reports that the space beneath the property measures 9,000 square feet and is scarcely visible as you arrive. The listing linked from its November 19 report identifies the site on Mount Larson Road and Frances Drive.

What the photograph reveals

Downstairs, the wood lining wraps from the walls onto the ceiling. Narrow strips of light follow the ceiling panels, and the floor catches their reflections beneath a row of four cars. The elevator door at the edge of the frame supplies a useful reference for the height of the room. Seen together, those details give a visitor a clearer sense of the space than the square footage alone. Want to see your property here?
