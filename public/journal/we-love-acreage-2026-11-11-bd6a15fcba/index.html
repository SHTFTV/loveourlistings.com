We Love This Listing: In Fischer, a recording room is built into the Hill Country

At 3711 FM 484, the musicians can sleep on the property and work in a live room set into the earth.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

With thanks

Coldwell Banker Realty

First published by Fancy Pants Homes. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

What the studio walls do

The most consequential detail at Studios at Fischer is hard to spot in a photograph. The room where musicians play is built into the ground, with concrete walls a foot thick. The floor inside it floats. Both choices make sense on a property where a stray vibration can spoil a take. The control room has quite a different job. Racks of equipment sit within reach of the engineer, who can choose how the sound gets captured.

Staying for the session

Fancy Pants Homes reported on November 11 that Coldwell Banker Realty had listed the 8.25-acre Texas property for $12 million. It includes overnight accommodation for musicians as well as a production soundstage. Someone working late can leave the recording room and stay on the same grounds.
