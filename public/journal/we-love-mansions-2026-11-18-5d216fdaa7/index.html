We Love This Listing: Concrete and water frame a Paradise Valley home

A double-sided fireplace gives the open living room a centre in Jack DeBartolo’s single-level Paradise Valley house.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

With thanks

Andrew Cahill Hoppin, as credited by Robb Report · native listing photograph via Compass / ARMLS

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Around the fireplace

In the living room of this Paradise Valley house, curved lounge chairs gather in front of a fireplace built into a broad concrete wall. Horizontal ridges catch the light above the opening. Look to the left of the hearth and you can see into the next room. The wall gives the seats a place of their own while keeping the rest of the house in sight.

Across one level

Robb Report credits Arizona architect Jack DeBartolo with the design. Completed in 2015, the house has a U-shaped plan spread across one level. Polished concrete floors, cold-rolled steel and wood recur through the interiors. A water feature marks the approach outside. Kevin Durant’s asking price was $13.9 million in the November 18 report. What stays with us after seeing the photographs is the scale of that hearth: tall enough to anchor a large room, with the fire at the height of someone seated beside it.
