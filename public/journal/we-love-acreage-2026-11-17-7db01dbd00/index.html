We Love This Listing: A wall of arched windows brings the garden to Hillandale’s pool

At this Stamford estate, a swimmer can look out through a line of full-height arches towards the trees.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

With thanks

Daniel Milstein for Sotheby’s International Realty via ELLE Decor

First published by ELLE Decor. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

At the water’s edge

Along one side of the indoor pool, the windows rise almost from the paving and finish in semicircles just below the ceiling. Reflections carry green from the garden across the surface of the water. Daniel Milstein’s photograph gives a sense of the room’s length without a person in the frame. ELLE Decor reported on November 17 that the pool is Olympic-sized and that Sotheby’s International Realty was asking $75 million for Hillandale.

A map of the grounds

The property spans 268 acres near the New York state line. Hunt & Hunt designed the eight-bedroom main house, with its carved wood, plasterwork and stained glass. The formal gardens give the house a nearby destination on foot. A lake and four guest houses occupy other parts of the estate, and a hedge maze offers the rare walk deliberately designed to keep its destination out of view.
