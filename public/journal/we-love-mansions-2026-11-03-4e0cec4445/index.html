We Love This Listing: La Paloma’s restored Montecito character

A Montecito property story makes the case for character that survives a new chapter.

Published 2026-11-09. Original source date: 2026-11-03.

The story behind the house

Sotheby’s International Realty’s 2026-11-03 feature identifies an early twentieth-century design by Mary McLaughlin Craig and later restoration and expansion by Xorin Balbes. Those are source-reported architectural credits, not an inspection or an interview conducted by this magazine.

Why we love this listing

A grand house can make an entrance. The more interesting question is what remains inviting after that first impression. Here, the appeal is the relationship between inherited character and later decisions: a home can gain another chapter without treating its history as a decorative label.

Look beyond the first photograph

For a property like this, we would read circulation, garden connections and everyday rooms alongside the formal spaces. Restoration is a reason to look more closely, not a substitute for current property documents or a qualified review of condition. The original feature supplies the reported detail and credited photography.
