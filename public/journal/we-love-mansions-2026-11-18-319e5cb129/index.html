We Love This Listing: An Arcadia Compound with Room to Gather

A guest house, garden water features and an elevator give this Longley Way residence several distinct rhythms.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

A layered plan for company

Coldwell Banker Global Luxury’s November 18 report describes a six-bedroom Arcadia compound built by Franklin Properties in 2018. The home offers nearly 10,900 square feet on roughly 0.89 acres, with an asking price just below $7 million. A detached guest house makes the arrangement more varied than the main residence alone.

Moving through the house and garden

The brokerage describes a 28-foot marble foyer, formal gathering rooms and a secondary preparation kitchen. A private elevator connects the floors. Outdoors, a waterfall and koi pond sit alongside a pool, dining pavilions and a sport court. These are different kinds of shared space, from quiet pauses to larger gatherings.

Why we love this listing

The interesting idea is choice within one property. Separate guest accommodation and varied outdoor areas allow the plan to support company without making every activity compete for the same room.
