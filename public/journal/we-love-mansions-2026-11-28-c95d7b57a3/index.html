We Love This Listing: The Townhouse Behind a New York Screen Romance

A red-brick Upper West Side townhouse pairs a memorable film façade with a more layered residential story.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-28.

With thanks

Country Life · Alamy

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A supporting role, precisely defined

The appeal of 328 West 89th Street begins with recognition, but deserves a careful distinction. Country Life identifies its façade as the exterior of Kathleen Kelly’s apartment in You’ve Got Mail. The interiors were filmed on a New Jersey soundstage: this is the architectural backdrop, not the film’s furnished home.

Substance beyond the screen

According to Country Life, the townhouse was built in 1894 from red brick and limestone, measures 20 feet wide and extends across five storeys. Those particulars give the building a compelling identity beyond its cameo—a late-19th-century house with its own architectural credentials.

One address, several homes

Country Life describes 5,885 square feet divided into seven residences, including an owner’s duplex with wood panelling and a spiral staircase. That arrangement is an important counterpoint to the single-home fantasy a familiar screen address might inspire. Its reported residential configuration is part of the story, not a footnote.

A quieter kind of distinction

The article also reports a private garden and a position between West End Avenue and Riverside Drive. Editorially, the garden is the most intriguing counterweight to the famous façade: one feature belongs to the building’s public image, while the other suggests a more private dimension to its domestic appeal.
