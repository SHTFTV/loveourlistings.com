We Love This Listing: Cloud House’s Colourful Take on London Art Deco

In Gospel Oak, a Miami-inspired home makes a case for colour that goes far beyond the front door.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-26.

With thanks

Country Life · UK Sotheby's International Realty

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

London, with a Miami accent

Country Life describes Cloud House as a pair of three-storey, semi-detached homes in Gospel Oak. The feature focuses on architect Peter Morris and his wife Emily’s half. Its pink façade, arches and turquoise-framed windows suggest an appealing alternative to the capital’s more restrained residential palette.

Personal rather than predictable

Morris tells Country Life that the couple’s Miami honeymoon informed the design, particularly its Art Deco curves and colours. That personal starting point is what interests us: the house reads as a deliberate expression of its owners’ tastes, rather than an exercise in pleasing everyone.

Colour with a counterpoint

According to the magazine, much of the ground floor accommodates an open-plan kitchen, dining and living space, alongside a study. Scalloped cabinets and oversized stripes continue the exuberance inside. Yet the reported white bedrooms offer a counterpoint—a useful reminder that bold design needn’t maintain the same intensity everywhere.

A rooftop finishing touch

Country Life reports a striped roof-terrace floor and an outdoor bathtub with a statement tap. For us, that final flourish makes the design feel coherent: the playful approach isn’t confined to the street-facing elevation, but carries through to a more private outdoor space.
