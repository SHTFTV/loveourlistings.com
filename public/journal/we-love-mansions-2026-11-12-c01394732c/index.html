We Love This Listing: An indoor pool opens to the lawn on Belvedere Island

On the western slope of Belvedere Island, 337 Belvedere Avenue puts the swimming pool and the lawn on speaking terms.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

With thanks

Concierge Auctions

First published by Concierge Auctions. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Two ways to enjoy the view

There are plenty of places to look out across San Francisco Bay at 337 Belvedere Avenue. The one that caught our eye was the swimming pool. Concierge Auctions describes a 60-foot indoor pool that opens to the lawn. A swimmer gets the shelter of a room and can step directly outside afterward. A second kitchen on the recreation level makes it easy to imagine spending an afternoon down there.

A hillside in the evening

The six-bedroom house was built in 2008 on a little more than half an acre. Its terraces face the afternoon and evening light, with views toward the bridge and the city. In the auctioneer’s photograph, the skyline is a narrow silhouette beyond a broad sweep of bay. Concierge Auctions announced on November 12 that bidding was scheduled to open December 4.
