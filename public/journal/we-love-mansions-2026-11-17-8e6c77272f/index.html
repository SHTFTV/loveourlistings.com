We Love This Listing: Carved timber surrounds the table at 9 East 88th Street

In this Manhattan house just off Fifth Avenue, a bank of windows lights the wood-panelled dining room.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

With thanks

jamel via ELLE Decor

First published by ELLE Decor. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The room around the table

In the photograph of the dining room, long curtains hang beside a row of windows on one side. Across the room, a pale fireplace surround interrupts the wood panelling. With a single round table in the centre, it is easy to see how far the daylight travels across the floor. ELLE Decor reported a $35 million asking price for the Upper East Side property on November 17.

Seven levels above the street

The house dates to 1902. James R. Turner and William G. Killian were responsible for the building, while John Russell Pope designed its interiors. Brick, limestone and marble make up the exterior. Indoors, three staircases serve an eight-bedroom residence spanning 14,100 square feet. An elevator reaches six of its seven levels. Higher up, a pair of roof terraces gives the residents a second setting for a meal, away from the formal table downstairs.
