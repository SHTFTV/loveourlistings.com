We Love This Listing: A Wayzata Estate Composed Around the Lake

At 372 Ferndale Road South, a broad shoreline connects the main house, porch, terraces and boathouse.

Published 2026-11-18. Original source date: 2026-11-07.

A generous edge on Wayzata Bay

Coldwell Banker Global Luxury’s November 7 feature describes a 3.51-acre estate with 330 feet of east-facing Lake Minnetonka shoreline. Designed by Peter Eskuche and completed in 2012, the 12,420-square-foot residence is reported at an asking price of $26.5 million. Water views are a recurring theme across the plan.

Several ways to spend a lakeside day

The brokerage describes a kitchen opening to a three-season porch with a stone fireplace, a main-floor primary wing and separate carriage-house guest accommodation. Outside, the pool, terraces and boathouse create different points of contact with the setting. Geothermal heating and a generator are among the reported supporting systems.

Why we love this listing

The appeal is the continuity between a substantial house and the smaller places where a day might unfold. A porch, a waterside terrace and a boathouse each offer a distinct relationship with the lake. That variety makes the shoreline more than a backdrop. Want to see your property here?
