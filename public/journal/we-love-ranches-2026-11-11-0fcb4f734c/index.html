We Love This Listing: Creston Manor puts five residences to work on a California ranch

Vineyard, pastures, stables and five homes share nearly 578 acres in San Luis Obispo County.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

A ranch read from the air

In the auctioneer’s aerial photograph, Creston Manor looks like a patchwork. Trees trace the edges of fields and a cluster of buildings occupies only a small part of the valley. Looking at it that way explains the appeal of this California property better than counting bedrooms. The vineyard and winery are part of the estate, as are the barns, stables and open pasture.

Several places to stay

Concierge Auctions traces the ranch to 1900 and describes five residences and a separate private suite. A group staying here could spread out across different buildings while sharing the working landscape around them. The November 11 announcement identifies Alex Trebek as a former owner and says bidding was scheduled to open December 4.
