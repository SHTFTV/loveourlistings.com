We Love This Listing: The Quaives keeps a working garden beside a musician’s retreat

Christine McVie’s former country home near Canterbury has three cottages, a greenhouse and roughly 18 acres of contrasting garden spaces.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

Where the garden meets the house

The kitchen at The Quaives has doors into the garden, while the drawing room opens to a south-facing terrace. Those are two quite different ways out of the same house. One suggests crossing the threshold with muddy boots and something just picked. The other suggests pulling up a chair and staying a while. A kitchen garden and a 35-foot greenhouse give that distinction some substance.

Room across the estate

The Spaces reported on November 18 that the Grade II-listed house near Canterbury was offered at £3 million through Harding Green. Christine McVie bought it in 1990 and lived there for 25 years. Seven bedrooms are arranged across three floors in the main house, and the estate includes three cottages.
