We Love This Listing: One roof for one Tribeca penthouse

The roof at 427 Washington Street covers the building’s footprint. A former industrial neighbourhood makes an unusually good backdrop for a garden.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

The block from above

Look at the listing photograph from above and you can trace the outline of the roof garden against the streets below. This is the private outdoor space for the penthouse at 427 Washington Street. Robb Report puts it at roughly 4,600 square feet. A water tower shares the roof with raised planting beds and places to sit. The neighbouring rooftops give the garden a distinctly New York horizon.

The floor below

Downstairs, the apartment takes a different approach to the same view. Eighteen windows face in four directions. A 48-foot great room accommodates both dining and lounging, and an alcove beside the dining area has room for a grand piano. The two bedrooms are part of the current arrangement. What draws us here is the freedom to step from a long, light-filled loft into a garden of comparable scale without crossing the street. Want to see your property here?
