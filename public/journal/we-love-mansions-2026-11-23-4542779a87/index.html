We Love This Listing: At a Montclair house, guests arrive on the middle floor

The terraces at 5606 La Salle Avenue give several rooms a share of the view over Oakland and San Francisco Bay.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Fusion Media via The Week

First published by The Week US. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Finding the front door

Seen from the drive, this six-bedroom Oakland house looks like a tall stack of terraces. The garage is down at the bottom, beneath the broad curve of a balcony. Guests enter a floor above it. The agent’s particulars place the foyer on the middle level, with three bedrooms upstairs and three more downstairs. That leaves the rooms where people gather together in the middle of the plan.

Behind the dining rooms

A staircase and an elevator connect the floors. On the main level, the kitchen sits between the dining areas, with three dishwashers and a separate butler’s pantry. A host clearing the table has a shorter trip than anyone carrying a plate to one of the terraces.
