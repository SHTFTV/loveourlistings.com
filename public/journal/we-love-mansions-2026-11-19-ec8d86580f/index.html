We Love This Listing: Queen Hoo Hall looks across the Lea valley

An Elizabethan house near Tewin makes room for a former great hall and a music room facing the garden.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

A house oriented to the view

At Queen Hoo Hall in Hertfordshire, a window is a good place to begin. Country Life describes a tower bay in the dining room, where stone floors and an inglenook fireplace draw the eye back indoors. The house stands above the Lea valley. Seventeen acres of gardens, paddocks and meadow give that outlook a foreground as well as a horizon.

Rooms with a second life

The former Great Hall now serves as a drawing room. A music room looks onto the formal gardens, while five bedrooms occupy the upper floors. We love how those changes of use leave the older house legible. A visitor can follow the line of the stonework or look closely at the surviving wall paintings without needing a floor plan to explain what makes the building distinctive.
