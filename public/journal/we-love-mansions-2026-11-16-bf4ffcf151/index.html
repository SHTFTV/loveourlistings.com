We Love This Listing: A straight garden path leads to a gilded dining room in Winnetka

At 919 Hill Road, a formal approach across the lawn sets the tone for a six-bedroom house built in 1929.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

With thanks

Christie’s International Real Estate / Luxury Portfolio International via The Week

First published by The Week US. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

The walk to the door

A straight gravel path divides the lawn at this Winnetka house. Pairs of planted urns mark the way in. The front door sits on the same line, though the building extends to either side and a low wing reaches out across the right of the photograph. Seen from the end of the path, the layout makes a large house surprisingly easy for a first-time visitor to read.

Two rooms for a shared meal

The Week featured the 1929 house on November 16. In the dining room, gilded mouldings catch the light and French doors break up the walls. The kitchen has seating of its own, so a smaller group can eat together without setting the formal table. A lacquered blue family room and several guest suites occupy other parts of the renovated house. The magazine reported an asking price of $7,999,900, including 1.25 acres of grounds.
