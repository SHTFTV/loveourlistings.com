We Love This Listing: Ancliff Down brings a reservoir into domestic use

A Wiltshire hillside conceals a former water reservoir with an upstairs living room and roughly two acres of landscaped grounds.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-13.

With thanks

Savills via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Where the rooms sit

Walk into most houses and the bedrooms are upstairs. Ancliff Down reverses the expectation. Country Life places all three bedrooms on the ground floor of this converted Wiltshire reservoir. The main living room and a study area are upstairs. That distinction tells us more about the conversion than the underground address alone. It gives the quieter rooms and the shared ones different places within an unusual structure.

Beyond the reservoir walls

The conversion dates to the late 1990s. Around the house are roughly two acres of landscaped grounds, with Bradford-on-Avon about three miles away. Country Life reported a £1.5 million asking price in its November 13 selection and attributed the listing to Savills. What we love here is the chance to read an engineering structure as a home, then step outside and see how much of it the hillside conceals. Want to see your property here?
