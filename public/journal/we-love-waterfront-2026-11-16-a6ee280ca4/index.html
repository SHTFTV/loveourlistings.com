We Love This Listing: Bosham Channel runs along the Old Town Hall garden

A stone wall marks the edge of a south-facing garden at this West Sussex house dating to 1694.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

A view across the channel

Seen from the water, the Old Town Hall sits behind a low stone wall. The dormers break up a steep roof, and the colour of the stone repeats in the wall along the shore. The south-facing garden runs beside Bosham Channel, with paved terraces and enough lawn to enjoy the water from farther back. On November 16, The Spaces reported that Finlay Brewer was seeking offers above £3.25 million for the Grade II-listed house.

Which room gets the view

The dining room occupies a later extension with a vaulted ceiling. French doors open towards the water, giving the table a connection to the channel even when the garden is too cold for lunch. Older details survive elsewhere, including flagstone floors and a carved lintel above the inglenook. Five bedrooms fill the upper levels, while a separate staircase leads to an office. A flat above the detached garage provides another place to stay.
