We Love This Listing: A tiled hall introduces Hye House near Battle

An oak staircase links the entrance of this Georgian house to two floors of bedrooms.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

What a guest sees first

At Hye House the first room makes a strong impression before anyone has taken a coat off. The photograph shows a run of patterned floor tiles leading past red walls to the oak staircase. Strutt & Parker dates the house to 1744 and identifies the hall floor as original. Two reception rooms occupy the front of the building. Farther back, a conservatory and an orangery give the ground floor a different relationship with the garden.

A building with two routines

Country Life included the East Sussex property in its November 22 selection at £2.5 million and described its use as a wellness retreat. That history helps explain the commercial fittings in the kitchen and the gym or studio beyond the orangery. It also makes the route from kitchen to garden worth noticing.
