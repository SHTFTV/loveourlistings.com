We Love This Listing: The bay fills a living-room corner in Pacific Heights

Windows meet at the corner of 2770 Broadway’s living room. Elsewhere in this San Francisco house the Chamberlain family preserved a more private kind of passage.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Brian Kitts, as credited by Robb Report · native listing photograph via Neal Ward Properties

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A window seat for a whole room

There is an easy way to tell where to sit in the living room at 2770 Broadway. A low table sits in front of a sofa, and an upholstered bench leaves the view open, with San Francisco Bay in view from either side. The windows turn a corner, letting someone seated at the end of the sofa look along the waterfront. The family also had a more secluded spot to play. Their daughter Kathryn remembered a door hidden behind a bookcase in the foyer that opened a shortcut to the kitchen.

What survived the renovation

David and Karin Chamberlain hired architect Jim Jennings and Van Acker Construction when they returned to Pacific Heights in 2007. Their project strengthened the foundation and gave the four floors an elevator. Patterned oak flooring and a leaded-glass skylight survived the work.
