We Love This Listing: Heavy ceiling beams lend a Fair Oaks sitting room its scale

White walls and dark timber frame the living spaces on this 8.2-acre estate near Sacramento.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Nick Sadek Sotheby’s International Realty via Mansion Global Listings

First published by Realtor.com. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

How to furnish a tall room

In the listing photograph, two dark ceiling beams cross above a white sofa and a grand piano. A deep fireplace opening sits low enough to be seen from the chairs, while tall windows admit the green of the trees. That contrast is part of the appeal of this Fair Oaks house. The beams give the room height, and the low hearth gives the people sitting there a place to gather.

What the asking price covers

The house dates from 1984 and draws on Santa Barbara’s Spanish-style architecture, with a red tile roof and white stucco outside. Among the tall trees on its 8.2 acres are a pool, tennis court and separate casita. Realtor.com reported on November 23 that Gavin Newsom and Jennifer Siebel Newsom had reduced the asking price from $7.5 million to $6.95 million.
