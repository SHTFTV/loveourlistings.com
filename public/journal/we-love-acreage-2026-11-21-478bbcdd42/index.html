We Love This Listing: Two pavilions frame the garden at Stoke Park

At Stoke Bruerne in Northamptonshire, matching buildings on either side of a pool lead remarkably different lives.

Published 2026-11-21. Original source date: 2026-11-21.

With thanks

Strutt & Parker via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Reading the garden front

Look across the water in the Strutt & Parker photograph and the two stone pavilions appear to balance each other perfectly. The house where a family would spend most of its time sits farther back. Country Life featured this 113-acre estate on November 21, reporting a guide price of £5.25 million. The River Tove marks its southern edge.

A library and a chapel

The west pavilion once held a library and later served as a ballroom. More recently it has hosted weddings. Across the garden, the east pavilion began as a chapel and now contains a two-bedroom residence. Those histories explain how one building could host a crowd while its near twin could accommodate someone for the night. Behind them, the four-bedroom Victorian house has its own connection with the grounds. Its library and study open to the garden, giving the family a quieter route outside.
