We Love This Listing: Denes House opens from a village street to a valley view

In Burwash, a white Georgian frontage hides a house whose principal rooms turn toward the High Weald.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-20.

With thanks

Blue Book via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A short walk from the gate

Denes House meets the High Street through a walled garden and an iron gate. Blue Book describes a fanlight over the entrance and a skylight over the flagstoned hall. That second source of daylight matters. A visitor can step away from the bright front windows and still follow the curve of the staircase by natural light. Country Life pictured the house in its November 20 feature and reported an asking price of £1.75 million.

How the back of the house works

The owners made the old kitchen into a sitting room and built a kitchen and breakfast room at the rear. The drawing room also opens south, through French windows onto a terrace overlooking the garden. Those details give the village house two distinct outlooks. The street is close enough to see from the front gate, while the rooms where people gather look out across the valley. Want to see your property here?
