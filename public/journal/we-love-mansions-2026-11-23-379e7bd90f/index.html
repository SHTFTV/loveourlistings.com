We Love This Listing: The screening room off Mulholland Drive was once Warren Beatty’s office

Two projection screens from different chapters of the house help explain how a family home became a place to show films.

Published 2026-11-28. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Cruzio Studios via ELLE Decor

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

From office to cinema

In the Cruzio Studios photograph, rows of purple seats face a screen almost as wide as the wall. This seventeen-seat room was Warren Beatty’s home office when he and Annette Bening lived here. Jonathan and Jenny Baker bought the house from the couple in 2012. During their renovation, the Bakers turned the office into a cinema and named it after its former occupant.

A second screen in the living room

Beatty’s original drop-down screen still has a place in the living room. Where the cinema can keep its lights low, the main gathering rooms open through sliding glass doors towards the canyon and the grounds. Between the formal dining room and kitchen, bottles hang on wires inside a glass wine room. Robb Report featured the Mulholland Drive property on November 23 and reported an asking price of $14.995 million.
