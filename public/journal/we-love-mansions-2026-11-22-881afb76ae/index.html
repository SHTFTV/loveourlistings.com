We Love This Listing: The coach house has its own front door at Brook House

A detached Victorian coach house lets working life occupy a separate building in Great Barford.

Published 2026-11-22. Original source date: 2026-11-22.

Behind the formal front

Brook House presents a remarkably even face to the garden. A dark entrance door sits between tall windows, and a balustrade runs across the roofline. Michael Graham describes a flagstone hall behind the door. The two front rooms open off that hall, while the kitchen, dining room and garden room connect towards the back. Upstairs, the five double bedrooms all belong to the main house.

The short commute across the grounds

The former coach house stands apart from the Grade II listed residence on Addingtons Road. It now has its own kitchen and bathroom, and the agent describes its two principal rooms as an office and a studio. That arrangement gives someone working from home an unusual luxury.
