We Love This Listing: Two barns give Higher Hutcherleigh room for company

Separate doors and kitchens divide the accommodation on this 20-acre property near Blackawton.

Published 2026-11-21. Original source date: 2026-11-15.

With thanks

Strutt & Parker

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Start in the yard

From above, Higher Hutcherleigh is a cluster of roofs gathered around a pale yard, with hedges dividing the fields beyond. That view helps explain an otherwise confusing bedroom count. Strutt & Parker puts five bedrooms in the principal house, one in the Coach House and three in Dukes Barn. Someone staying for a week could cook and sit down to dinner independently in either of the converted barns.

Follow the stove

Inside the principal house, a double-sided wood stove serves a sitting room on either side. The cooking and dining happen elsewhere, in a kitchen about 34 feet long with two islands. A sunroom next door gives people a way out to the garden without having to thread between the cook and the dining table.
