We Love This Listing: Schindler's Lovell Beach House comes up for sale

On the sand at Newport Beach, the 1926 house RM Schindler designed for Dr Philip Lovell is listed for $18.8 million. These photographs are of that house.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-12.

With thanks

Photography courtesy of The Lovell Beach House, via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

On the sand

The Spaces reported that the Lovell Beach House, designed by RM Schindler in 1926, is for sale for the first time in its history. It stands on the sand at Newport Beach and is listed for $18.8 million. The house has stayed in the same family since it was built for naturopath Dr Philip Lovell and his wife Leah.

The frame

Schindler set the house on five reinforced-concrete frames so the rooms look straight at the Pacific. A double-height living room faces the ocean through a wall of glass, and a cantilevered balcony connects four bedrooms above. Original Schindler furniture remains, including a built-in sofa and shelving between the living and dining rooms. Sleeping porches were enclosed by Schindler in 1928 after the coastal fog proved too cold.

The record

The house has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1974. The asking price and the photographs belong to the listing account published by The Spaces. Current availability is not independently confirmed.
