We Love This Listing: At Glenbruach, save the room under the eaves for last

Near Brig o’Turk, a low window under a pitched roof gives the top floor its own view of Loch Achray.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Savills via Country Life

First published by Country Life. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

One flight farther

Seven of Glenbruach’s eight bedrooms share the first floor. The eighth requires another climb, and Country Life’s November 23 photographs explain the appeal. Deep panes of glass follow the slope of the roof almost to the floor. From a seat alongside them the view runs straight across to the wooded hills. The bedroom up here has its own sitting room, dressing room and bathroom, enough space for someone to keep a different rhythm from the rest of the house.

Two versions of a winter afternoon

Downstairs, three windows face the loch from the family room beside the kitchen. Farther indoors, the cinema offers another way to spend a dark afternoon. The Dunsmure family commissioned the house in the 1870s. Country Life reports a substantial renovation in 2012 and an asking price of £2.35 million for this house and its grounds near Brig o’Turk.
