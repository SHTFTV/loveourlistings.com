We Love This Listing: Kirkby Park

Near Douglas, a winding drive and 48 acres give this Isle of Man country house an unusually long introduction.

Published 2026-11-19. Original source date: 2026-11-08.

The approach

In Knight Frank’s photograph of Kirkby Park, a bend in the driveway brings the entrance into view between a flowering shrub and a sweep of lawn. The red door is easy to pick out against the pale facade. It gives the eye somewhere to land before you start counting windows.

Space around the house

Country Life featured the property on November 8 at £8 million through Knight Frank. Its setting near Douglas includes 48 acres. That number makes more sense when you look again at the approach. Mature planting screens part of the house even this close to the front door, and the driveway still has room to turn.

Why we love this listing

A grand arrival can feel surprisingly personal when the last thing you see is a single bright door. That is the detail we would remember at Kirkby Park. Want to see your property here?
