We Love This Listing: The Great Stair & Library at Burley on the Hill

A four-storey residence within a Baroque country house, where a painted staircase takes centre stage.

Published 2026-11-12. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

An apartment on a different scale

Country Life describes The Great Stair & Library at Burley on the Hill as a four-storey, 12,000sq ft apartment. That combination makes the architectural proposition unusually interesting: the scale of a mansion, but within a divided historic house rather than a standalone estate.

A staircase with a story

According to Country Life, the Great Stair rises from the ground floor to the first floor amid murals telling the story of Perseus and Andromeda. The publication attributes them to Gerard Lanscroon, dating their creation to 1708–1712. Here, circulation becomes the central architectural event rather than a supporting space.

Grandeur, with shared boundaries

The distinction between private rooms and shared amenities matters. Country Life reports direct, non-exclusive access to a ballroom, tapestry hall and garden saloon, alongside 67 acres of communal grounds. Our reading: the attraction is participation in a larger country-house setting, not sole possession of everything it contains.

A substantial second chapter

Country Life credits architect Kit Martin with converting the house, outbuildings and stables into 23 homes in the 1990s. It also identifies this residence as the largest of the main house’s six homes and the only one retaining its original state rooms intact. That continuity gives the conversion its particular editorial interest.
