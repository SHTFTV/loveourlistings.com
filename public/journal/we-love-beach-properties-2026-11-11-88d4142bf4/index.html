We Love This Listing: From the front lawn, a straight line to the Atlantic in Vero Beach

At 2120 South Highway A1A, the long approach and low roofline leave the ocean horizon visible from the landward side of the house.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-11.

With thanks

Concierge Auctions

First published by Concierge Auctions. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

On the approach

A paved path cuts through the lawn and lines up with a tall central bay. From this angle in the auctioneer’s photograph, you can see sky and ocean above the roof before you can see the front door. The grounds run from Highway A1A to the beach. Concierge Auctions gives their area as about 3.15 acres and reports more than 223 feet of direct ocean frontage.

Room for visitors

Completed in 2021, the main residence pairs Alabama limestone floors with walnut walls and doors. A separate two-bedroom guest house allows visitors to keep their own hours. The outdoor kitchen and saltwater pool give the two households somewhere to meet without going through the main living rooms.
