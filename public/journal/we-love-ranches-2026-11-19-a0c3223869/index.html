We Love This Listing: Fir columns divide the car collection at a Reno ranch

At 515 Rhodes Road, the basement garage gives a collection of cars and motorcycles an unusual set of neighbours: the heavy timber posts that hold up the building.

Published 2026-11-23. Original source date: 2026-11-19.

With thanks

Sierra Sotheby’s International Realty via Sotheby’s International Realty

First published by Sotheby’s International Realty. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A ranch detail below stairs

A car leaves quite a different impression standing next to the trunk of a tree than it does on the floor of a showroom. In the garage at this Reno ranch, the posts retain their irregular outlines. A red coupe sits between two of them, and motorcycles occupy the bay nearest the camera. The wood gives a good sense of scale, even in a photograph taken close to the vehicles.

How the collection fits

Sotheby’s International Realty featured the Nevada property in its November 19 survey of garages. The report places this one in the basement and identifies the timber as old-growth fir. Look past the vehicles to the far wall in the accompanying photograph. Windows extend along that side, bringing daylight to a room whose underground position otherwise tells you very little about how it feels inside. Want to see your property here?
