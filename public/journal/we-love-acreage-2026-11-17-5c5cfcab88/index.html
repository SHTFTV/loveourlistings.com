We Love This Listing: A series of stone arches connects the rooms on Friar Tuck Lane

In Houston’s Sherwood Forest, a broad arch lets a sitting room keep its own shape while opening to the kitchen.

Published 2026-11-26. Original source date: 2026-11-17.

With thanks

Coldwell Banker Realty Houston

First published by Fancy Pants Homes. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Where one room ends

There is a good place to see the plan of this Houston house without a drawing. Stand in the sitting room and look past the pale sofa. A deep stone arch fills most of the space between two walls. The kitchen is visible through it, with another opening beyond. The stone gives each room a clear edge, while the people in one room can still see what is happening in the next.

Rooms at garden level

Built in 2008, the house at 327 E. Friar Tuck Lane sits on 1.73 acres. Two of the six bedrooms are on the ground floor, including the primary suite with a sitting area overlooking the grounds. The garden has covered places to eat, a summer kitchen and a heated pool with a spa.
