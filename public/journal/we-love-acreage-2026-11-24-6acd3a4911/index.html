We Love This Listing: A glass winter garden opens the roof at Maison H

Bruno Albert put the living rooms upstairs in this Belgian house, where the slope brings the treetops close.

Published 2026-11-24. Original source date: 2026-11-24.

With thanks

ArchitectenWoning via The Spaces

First published by The Spaces. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Looking up from the garden

From the grounds of Maison H, the roof looks almost big enough to be the house. One end opens into a rounded cage of glass, with an outdoor stair running down from it. In Esneux, architect Bruno Albert took advantage of the hillside by putting the private rooms on the lower level. A timber spiral staircase leads up to the kitchen, library and spaces for eating and sitting. The light from the winter garden reaches into those rooms.

Art built into the rooms

The house took shape between 1980 and 1985. Gérald Dederen contributed to the barrel vault over the shared spaces, while Léon Wuidar designed sliding panels with geometric patterns. Someone moving a panel changes the room and the artwork in the same gesture. Jean-Pierre Herzet’s sculptures extend that collaboration into the wooded grounds. The Spaces reported a €1.195 million asking price through ArchitectenWoning on November 24.
