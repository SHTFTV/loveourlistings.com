We Love This Listing: a Beverly Hills mansion built around performance

A private nightclub and recording studio give this Trousdale Estates home a distinctive entertainment brief.

Published 2026-11-11. Original source date: 2026-11-03.

An unusually specific idea of home

Realtor.com’s November 3 feature describes an eight-bedroom Trousdale Estates mansion listed for $70 million. Its entertainment spaces include a private nightclub with a DJ booth and dance floor, a professional recording studio, and a screening room. The matching broker listing identifies the property as 1181 North Hillcrest Road in Beverly Hills. These are dated source-reported details, rather than a new inspection or valuation.

Why we love this listing

The interesting question is how a home accommodates sound, performance and a gathering of people. A room for making music serves a different purpose from one for listening, dancing or watching a film. Here, those activities have their own places. That makes the entertainment program a coherent design story, rather than simply a long list of luxury extras.

The quieter view

The clean exterior photograph comes from the property’s current Christie’s listing. It places the house and its pool against the surrounding hillside, offering a calmer counterpoint to the interior’s reported party spaces. We like that contrast: a residence can have a theatrical personality while still being read through its setting. Follow the original feature and property listing for the full reported details and present terms.
