We Love This Listing: Le Paradis looks out over Cinnamon Bay

Tile roofs and tropical foliage sit below the horizon at this hillside estate on St. John.

Published 2026-11-20. Original source date: 2026-11-16.

The roofs above the bay

In the auctioneer’s photograph of Le Paradis, the rooftops occupy a surprisingly small part of the scene. Trees climb almost to the eaves, and the bright arc of Cinnamon Bay appears below the house. That is the distinction worth making about this St. John address. It offers a view from above the beach, with enough elevation to see how the shoreline bends around the forest.

A place to pause

Concierge Auctions describes an eight-bedroom residence on an acre, with a palm-lined approach and a saltwater pool. Inside, polished sycamore cabinetry and a kitchen island of tiger’s eye give people at the counter something to look at even with the view behind them. The auctioneer’s November 16 announcement scheduled bidding to open December 8. We would start a tour on the terrace, then walk back into the kitchen to see how the light changes.
