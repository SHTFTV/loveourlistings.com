We Love This Listing: A garden separates two very different rooms on East 91st Street

A detached, wood-lined carriage house shares the bluestone garden of a six-story Carnegie Hill townhouse.

Published 2026-11-27. Original source date: 2026-11-18.

With thanks

Jon Nissenbaum / @instaniss via Realtor.com

First published by Robb Report. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

Across the garden

The carriage house at 168 East 91st Street has the scale of a sitting room with the height of a small hall. Narrow boards of Douglas fir climb the walls, and a row of high windows admits light above the furniture. A ladder in the photograph gives a sense of how far the sleeping loft is from the floor. With the main house across the garden, a guest can retire here without taking an entire floor of the townhouse.

Two views of the same courtyard

French doors at garden level open onto bluestone paving. From upstairs, a terrace outside the third-floor den looks back towards the carriage house. Those two viewpoints help explain why a detached building can feel close even in a six-story residence. Robb Report covered the listing on November 18.
