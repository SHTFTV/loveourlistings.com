We Love This Listing: A tree sets the course for arrival at an Oakland Hills manor

At the Hillcrest Estates house on Skyline Boulevard, the drive circles a mature tree before it reaches the front door.

Published 2026-11-25. Original source date: 2026-11-23.

With thanks

Vanguard Properties / Luxury Portfolio International via The Week

First published by The Week US. Love Our Listings wrote this feature from that account.

A tree at the front door

The open gates frame a broad tree in a ring of paving. Its crown spreads across the middle of the house, so it takes a moment to pick out the front door behind it. The wings on either side are easier to see. Their low eaves bring the scale of the building down at the courtyard edge, something that matters on a property large enough to have a six-car garage.

What fits behind the courtyard

The Week featured the six-bedroom Oakland house on November 23, reporting an asking price of $2.985 million. Built in 1993, the house takes advantage of its hillside setting with views across San Francisco Bay. A few steps down into the living room, a vaulted ceiling gives extra height above the fireplace and French doors.
